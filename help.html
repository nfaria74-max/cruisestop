Common Questions

Do I need internet during the route?

No. Once you open the route page, the main content works offline. Maps and navigation need internet.

Can I share the route with my travel partner?

Yes. Share the link and they can open it on their phone too.

What if I am running late?

Follow the Back to Ship timer. If it turns red, stop the route and head back immediately.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If you are not happy, contact us on WhatsApp within 24 hours and we will refund you.

How long does access last?

30 days on the device used to unlock the route.

The route is not opening. What do I do?

Refresh the page. If the problem continues, contact us on WhatsApp and we will help.
