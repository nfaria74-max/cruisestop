CruiseStop is not for cruise booking or broad travel content. This page describes a digital self-guided walking route in Funchal for cruise passengers who want a scenic route during a short port stop.

PANORAMIC is made for travellers who want the city to open up quickly: blue water, rooftops, old streets and easy orientation from the cruise port. It is a structured Funchal cruise port walk, not a group tour, and it works directly in the browser on your phone.

The route is organised into five scenic moments. Each stop gives you a place name, suggested timing and simple navigation, while the Back to Ship timer keeps the real cruise deadline visible.
