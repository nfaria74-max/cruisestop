More about Panoramic Funchal

This route is designed for cruise passengers who want to see Funchal from its best angles. It focuses on sea views, rooftops, old streets and scenic pauses that help the city feel open and memorable. The idea is not to rush. It is to turn a short port call into a clear and enjoyable walk with enough structure to feel confident and enough freedom to enjoy the views.

Panoramic Funchal includes five sequential scenic moments. Each stop has a place name, a suggested time and a clear Continue or Skip choice. You can stop for photos, take in the bay a little longer or move on when you are ready. A Back to Ship timer stays part of the experience so the return to the cruise port never becomes a last-minute worry.

All routes start and finish within walking distance of the Funchal cruise port. That matters because a short cruise stop is not the time for complicated transfers. You can leave the ship, open the route on your phone and begin walking. No app download is required, and the route is made for use outdoors with clear buttons, high contrast and simple instructions.

Choose Panoramic Funchal if you want sea views, city texture and a broader visual feel of Funchal without pressure. It is best for travellers who want a more scenic stop with easy orientation.
