Start with Taste Madeira

Taste Madeira is built for the limited time of a cruise stop. Open the route on your phone, follow the food moments and explore Funchal without a long restaurant booking or a fixed group schedule.

The route is designed around local flavour rather than tourist traps. It points you toward food moments, streets and small pauses that help Funchal feel real. You can move at your own pace, stop when something looks good and keep the walk flexible. That makes it useful for couples, families and solo cruise passengers who want a taste of Madeira but still need a clear plan.

Taste Madeira is organised into five sequential moments. Each moment gives you a stop name, a suggested time and a simple Continue or Skip action. The route gives you enough direction to make good decisions while leaving space for appetite, weather and energy.

All routes start and finish within walking distance of the Funchal cruise port. That is important because the ship schedule is the real deadline. The route includes back-to-ship timing, clear outdoor-friendly buttons and phone-first pages that are easy to read in bright light. No app download is needed.

Choose Taste Madeira if your ideal Funchal stop includes local food, hidden streets and a more authentic feel than the first restaurant beside the port.
